# Data Policy and Provenance

## Scope

This repository contains code, documentation, and only small, approved example images and derived outputs. It does not redistribute the full manuscript corpus or the full set of generated line images.

## Source collection

The project uses material from the Julia Newberry diary collection available through the Newberry’s digital transcription resources: <https://digital.newberry.org/transcribe/>.

## Access and attribution

Users should obtain source material from the original collection or follow the access method approved by the project authors. Preserve source attribution in examples, visualizations, publications, and derivative material.

## Rights and responsible reuse

The Newberry states that collection images may be used for lawful purposes, commercial or non-commercial, without licensing or permission fees to the library. However, users are responsible for determining whether specific material is in the public domain, subject to copyright, culturally sensitive, or otherwise restricted. See <https://www.newberry.org/policies>.

Do not upload unreviewed scans, personally sensitive material, or data whose reuse conditions are uncertain. Direct questions about particular collection materials to the original collection or rights holder.
